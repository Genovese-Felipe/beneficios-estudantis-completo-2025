## Why "00-FUNDAMENTOS" Instead of "99-REGISTROS-HISTORICOS"?

**Original name**: `99-REGISTROS-HISTORICOS` (Historical Records)
**New name**: `00-FUNDAMENTOS` (Foundations)

**Reasoning**:
1. **Logical ordering**: These are foundational documents that inform everything else, so they should come first (00) not last (99)
2. **Better semantics**: "Foundations" better describes content that serves as the basis for the entire project
3. **Improved navigation**: Users naturally start at 00 when exploring numbered folders
4. **Consistency**: Aligns with standard practices where 00 represents base/core/foundation
